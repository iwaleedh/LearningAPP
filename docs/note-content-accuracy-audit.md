# Subject-note accuracy audit — 9 October 2026

## Scope and limits

The inventory covers all **2,055 seed notes in 15 subject folders**, including complete-reference blocks, recall content, and attached guided lessons. The catalogue received arithmetic triage and targeted searches for common misconceptions. Selected questionable claims were checked against original educational texts, standards bodies and exam-board materials. This is **not a line-by-line factual certification of every note**, every diagram label, every cited case study, or every historical/current statistic.

Confirmed corrections were made in the source notes, so generated guided steps and their self-check explanations inherit them. The 11 separately authored Chemistry foundation lessons were also inspected; their rounded calculation constants remain appropriate when stated as approximations.

## Repeatable checks

Run `node scripts/audit-note-content.mjs` to emit the inventory and potential simple-arithmetic discrepancies as JSON. The scanner understands common number grouping, superscripts and percentage notation. It skips SVG markup and HTML fraction layouts. It does not evaluate symbolic algebra, chemistry, diagrams or facts. Candidates are for review, never automatic replacement.

The final arithmetic run has eight remaining candidates, each inspected and explained by unsupported notation or deliberate context: the business “2+2=5” synergy metaphor, a symbolic solubility expression, a grouped exponent, a middle-dot product, a symbolic π result, two ratio calculations, and an HTML-entity scientific exponent. They are not confirmed arithmetic errors.

`src/data/noteContentAccuracy.test.js` independently recomputes corrected correlation, combustion, gas-law, modulus and radiation examples, and checks atom conservation in corrected equations. Existing catalogue-integrity tests check reachability, imports and diagram markup; these structural checks are not evidence of factual accuracy.

## Outstanding editorial review

- Remaining prose, recall answers and diagram labels need a lesson-by-lesson review against the matching syllabus and reliable subject references.
- Scraped Physics notes can contain truncated prose and missing notation beyond the confirmed examples above.
- Named-company case studies, country data, financial rules and unsourced statistics require dated source verification. No attempt was made to certify every time-sensitive claim.
- Practical numbering, teaching simplifications and assessment wording must be checked against each qualification’s current specification. Keep the exact exam-supplied constants where required.

## Validation result

All 166 unit tests pass, including the six new numerical/content regressions and catalogue-wide integrity checks. Application and pipeline lint (`npm run lint:all`) and the production build pass. `git diff --check` reports no whitespace errors. These checks validate the corrections and code integrity; they do not certify unreviewed prose.
