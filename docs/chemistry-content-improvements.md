# AL Chemistry content improvements

The content review covered the structure and lesson depth of all 208 AL Chemistry notes. It identified thin, diagram-heavy lessons and incorrect practical labels. This update revises 62 notes across all six units, with approximately 13,000 words of newly authored explanations, worked examples and cautions. It does not claim a complete independent fact check of every existing sentence or diagram in the remaining notes.

| Unit | Revised notes | Main improvements |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| WCH11 | 24 | Particle classification, a corrected water/lattice diagram, orbital model, all bonding notes, alkane foundations, E/Z and practical context |
| WCH12 | 20 | Standard states, Hess calculations, intermolecular forces, solvents, hydrates, ion tests, titrations, rates, equilibrium and organic tests |
| WCH13 | 3 | Practical methods, planning, uncertainty and directional error evaluation |
| WCH14 | 7 | Initial-rate deduction, rate-constant units, entropy, Kc, weak acids, buffers and chirality |
| WCH15 | 6 | Cell emf, cell investigation, coordination, benzene, amino-acid charge and combustion analysis |
| WCH16 | 2 | Advanced practical planning and identification from multiple tests |

Each revised lesson includes an explanation, worked application, common mistakes, a self-check checklist, a coherent final summary and four explicit recall answers. Existing diagrams and specialist material were preserved except where a correction was necessary. Note identifiers and URLs are unchanged.

Specific corrections include the malformed water representation, polyatomic-ion versus molecular terminology, universal solubility and melting-point claims, the calorimeter heat-capacity correction, orbital-boundary overstatements, and practical labels. Core Practical 2 is now identified with Hess’s law in Unit 2; 1-bromobutane preparation is labelled as a further suggested practical. The electrochemistry practical label reflects investigation of cells.

Syllabus and practical mapping were checked against the [Pearson IAL Chemistry 2018 specification](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20Advanced%20Level/Chemistry/2018/Specification-and-Sample-Assessment/International-A-Level-Chemistry-Spec.pdf) and [Pearson practical guide](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20Advanced%20Level/Chemistry/2018/Teaching-and-Learning-Materials/teacher-practical-guide.pdf). These are curriculum references; the added explanatory prose and illustrative numerical data are authored study material, not copied examination mark schemes. Preparation methods remain subject to the laboratory’s supplied procedure.

Validation passed: all 144 unit tests including syllabus/seed-note integrity, app lint, production build and direct rendering of all 62 revised notes through the HTML/SVG sanitiser. Additional content checks verified four non-empty recall answers per revised note, a final summary, no missing blocks, and representative worked calculations. No backend or production deployment was changed in this update.
